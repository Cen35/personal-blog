# PERSONAL BLOG design system

## Direction

Quiet letter: a personal blog presented as a carefully composed sheet of stationery. The writing is the primary material; small taped landscape fragments and handwritten margin notes balance the reading column.

## Palette

- Paper: `#E6E2CF`
- Mist blue: `#7A8F94`
- Pale grey: `#B6BDBA`
- Warm taupe ink: `#635B5B`
- Deep ink: derived from warm taupe, never pure black

## Typography

- Display and handwritten notes: LXGW WenKai
- Body and controls: Nunito Sans
- Two type families maximum

## Motion

Subtle. Page enter, staggered article rows, small paper drift, button press and menu/dialog transitions. Only `transform` and `opacity` move. Every motion has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback.

## Accessibility

All controls have visible focus states, labels and keyboard operation. Body text is at least 16px. Colour contrast is checked against the paper surface.
